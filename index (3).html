Why pray for priests

If we get to Heaven, it will be because of a priest

i

To save souls

It is the priest who preaches, baptises, counsels and absolves; through him graces flow to sinners. When a friend needs conversion, we send him to a priest. What better way to convert souls than to pray for the priests?

ii

To support and sanctify priests

Think of the weight and fatigue borne by priests who travel day and night to bring the Sacraments to scattered faithful, many caring single-handedly for a whole state or country. They remain men, open to temptation, and their fall scandalises many.

iii

To express gratitude

We owe thanks to them for serving tirelessly, and to God, who shows us undeserved mercy each time He gives us a priest. Our Blessed Mother saw her Son in every priest; St Thérèse gave her life in Carmel for them.

“There are no bad priests, only priests for whom there has not been enough prayer.”
St John Vianney

How the apostolate works

A hidden spiritual commitment

  1. Men and women of 18 years and over may join, provided they are baptised and can commit to the daily prayers.
  2. Each member receives the name of the priest they are to pray for, and the booklet of daily and monthly prayers.
  3. The apostolate stays hidden: priests are not told who prays for them.
  4. Each member makes the Act of Dedication and renews it once a year on the same date.
  5. If at any time you cannot continue, write to Mary, the coordinator, so a replacement can be found.

Priests associated with the apostolate offer Holy Mass and conduct a Holy Hour on the First Thursday of each month.

Prayer to Our Lady of Sorrows

Daily

Dear Mother Mary, grant me a most tender and compassionate love for Thy Divine Son and for Thine own Sorrows. Amen.

Prayers to the Holy Ghost

Daily

O Holy Ghost, beloved of my soul, I adore Thee. Enlighten me, guide me, strengthen me, console me. Tell me what I should do; give me Thine orders. I promise to submit myself to all that Thou desirest of me and to accept all that Thou permittest to happen to me. Let me only know Thy will. Amen.

Come Holy Ghost, Creator, come from Thy bright heavenly throne. Come take possession of my soul and make me all Thine own. Thou who art sevenfold in Thy gifts, finger of God’s right hand; His promise teaching little ones to know and understand. Amen.

O Divine Spirit of Light and Strength and of Love, enlighten my mind, that I may better understand true devotion to Mary, Thy Spouse and Mediatrix of All Graces, so that I may enlighten others. Strengthen my will, that I may practise this devotion of Thy servant St Louis-Marie de Montfort, so that I may teach more by example than by words. Inflame my heart with such burning love for Jesus and Mary that, as Their apostle, under Thy guidance, I may hasten the reign of Jesus through Mary in the hearts of all men. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

Prayer for Priests

Daily

O God, who didst love Thy Beloved Son from all eternity and didst will Him to become at once the Great High Priest and the Saving Victim for the sins of the whole world, pour forth Thy Holy Spirit, we beseech Thee, upon Thy servants the priests of His Holy Church, and grant that in these days of tribulation there may be an increase in the number and sanctity of vocations to the holy office of the priesthood, and a growing fervour on the part of all those who offer the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass and minister to the care of souls.

O Jesus our Great High Priest, may all the love and help and merciful compassion of Thy Sacred Heart be with Thy priests in their life and work, especially in the sacred ministry of the Altar. Graciously accept our prayers and communions in union therewith, and grant to Thy priests who have departed this life everlasting rest and the reward of their labours for the salvation of souls.

O Almighty, Eternal God, look upon the face of Thy Christ, and for love of Him, who is the Eternal High Priest, have pity on Thy priests. Remember, O most compassionate God, that they are but weak and frail human beings. Stir up in them the grace of their vocation which is in them by the imposition of the Bishop’s hands. Keep them close to Thee, lest the enemy prevail against them, so that they may never do anything in the slightest degree unworthy of their sublime vocation.

O Jesus, I pray Thee for Thy faithful and fervent priests; for Thy unfaithful and tepid priests; for Thy priests labouring at home or abroad in distant mission fields; for Thy tempted priests; for Thy lonely and desolate priests; for Thy young priests; for Thy aged priests; for Thy sick priests; for Thy dying priests; for the souls of Thy priests in Purgatory.

But above all I commend to Thee the priests dearest to me: the priest who baptised me; the priests who absolved me from my sins; the priests at whose Masses I assisted and who gave me Thy Body and Blood in Holy Communion; the priests who taught and instructed me, or helped me and encouraged me; all the priests to whom I am indebted in any other way, particularly N.. O Jesus, keep them all close to Thy Heart, and bless them abundantly in time and in eternity. Amen.

For my priest

O Jesus, Eternal Priest, keep N., Thy priest, within the shelter of Thy Sacred Heart, where none may touch him. Keep unstained his anointed hands, which daily hold Thy Sacred Body. Keep unsullied the lips purpled with Thy Precious Blood. Keep pure and unearthly a heart sealed with the sublime mark of Thy glorious Priesthood. Let Thy holy love surround him and shield him from the world’s contagion. Bless his labours with abundant fruit, and may they to whom he has ministered be here below his joy and consolation, and in Heaven his beautiful and everlasting crown. Amen.

Offering of the Precious Blood

May be said daily

My God, I offer this action of mine, and every action of my whole life, in union with the Precious Blood in all the Masses this hour being offered all over the world, or that will be offered for all time, to Thine infinite rejoicing and delight.

For the glory of the Sacred Heart; in honour of Our Blessed Lady, St Joseph, my Angel Guardian, my patron saint, St Michael and all the Angels and Saints; for all the interests of Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament; for the conversion of sinners and those in their last agony; for all whom I love and all who have asked my prayers; for all whom I have injured; for the Holy Souls; for my enemies; for all my intentions; in atonement for my sins and the sins of the whole world, and for all the wants of Holy Church: I offer Thee the Precious Blood, O my God!

Act of Dedication

Yearly

Almighty God, in union with the sacrifice of our Lord Jesus Christ on Calvary, and in union with the most Sorrowful and Immaculate Heart of Mary, I, your name, resign into Thy hands, O Mother, my life and my death as a sacrifice of love for the sanctification and temporal welfare of Thy Priests. To this end, O Almighty God, I beg Thee to grant me the grace of perseverance until death. Let me love Thee, O God. Let me love Thee and serve Thee faithfully all the days of my life. Amen.

Dear St Thérèse, my Patroness, pray for me and protect me always.

Renew this dedication at least once a year on the same date.

Daily

May God restrain him, we humbly pray; and do thou, O Prince of the heavenly host, by the power of God, thrust down into hell Satan and all the wicked spirits who wander through the world for the ruin of souls. Amen.

Priestly societies we pray for

SAJM

Society of the Apostles of Jesus and Mary

Société des Apôtres de Jésus et de Marie. A priestly society of common life without vows, founded by Bishop Faure on 22 August 2016, feast of the Immaculate Heart of Mary. Its statutes follow those written by Archbishop Lefebvre.

SSPX–MC

Society of St Pius X – Marian Corps

Formed by priests who left the Society from 2013, first known as the “Strict Observance”. Serves faithful in North America, India, East Asia and Oceania.

USML

Union Sacerdotale Marcel Lefebvre

The priestly union formed in France with the Dominicans of Avrillé and other French priests of the Resistance.

No priest is excluded from our prayers. We pray also for the Pope and the Hierarchy of Holy Mother Church.
